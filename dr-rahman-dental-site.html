iii. Dental implants

A new tooth, built in four stages.

i.

Examination & scan

An X-ray and examination check the bone and plan the exact angle and depth.

ii.

The implant

A small titanium post is placed under local anaesthetic. It becomes your new root.

iii.

Healing & abutment

Over the following weeks the bone bonds to the implant, then a connector is fitted.

iv.

Your crown

A custom, colour-matched crown completes the tooth. Fixed, natural, permanent.

Ask on WhatsApp
Does an implant hurt?

It's done under local anaesthetic, so you feel pressure rather than pain. Most patients find it easier than an extraction, and any soreness afterwards is usually managed with ordinary painkillers.

Am I too old for braces?

No. Teeth can be moved at any age if the gums and bone are healthy. Many of our orthodontic patients are adults.

How much will my treatment cost?

It depends on your case, so you'll receive a written estimate after the examination — with no obligation to proceed on the day.

Can I get an opinion before visiting?

Yes. Send clear photos of your teeth on WhatsApp for an initial view, then come in for a full examination.
